# History lens

For historical events, causal claims about the past, and arguments that use history as evidence.

## Where the tell hides

- **Periodisation.** Where the author starts and ends the story. Choosing the start date usually determines the conclusion, and is almost never argued for.
- **The counterfactual.** Any causal claim about the past implies an alternative that did not happen. Surface it and test whether it is plausible.
- **Whose account survives.** Literacy, record-keeping, archives, victors, and which institutions had reason to preserve documents. Absence of evidence in history is usually evidence about who could write.
- **The unit of analysis.** Nation, class, region, technology, climate. Switching the unit frequently reverses the conclusion.
- **Present-day argument being run backwards.** Much historical writing is contemporary politics in costume. Identify the current dispute the argument serves.
- **Numbers.** Pre-modern statistics are estimates built on estimates. Trace any figure to its actual basis.

## Bear case angles specific to this lens

- **Historiography.** What the current scholarly consensus is and whether this argument is inside or outside it. Say which historians hold what.
- **Selection.** Whether the cases cited are the whole set or the flattering subset. Ask what the counterexamples are.
- **Causation versus sequence.** Whether the argument establishes a mechanism or only an order of events.
- **Anachronism.** Whether modern categories (nation, race, capitalism, the economy) are being applied to periods that did not have them.
- **Determinism.** Whether contingency has been flattened into inevitability because we know the ending.
- **Scale mismatch.** Whether an argument proven at one scale is being applied at another.

## Reminder

Run the finance lens here too, and do it early. Who financed the war, who held the debt, what the tax base was, who owned the land, what the trade balance did. Most popular history underweights this badly, which is exactly why it is where a differentiated take tends to be available.
