# Checklist — grant / public funding

**Different output mode.** These instruments (France 2030, BPI, EU Horizon Europe,
EIC, EuroHPC) are largely **non-negotiable** — A&B complies rather than redlines.
So do **not** produce redlines. Produce an **obligations map**:

1. **What A&B is committing to** — the obligations and strings, in plain English.
2. **Conflict flags** — where an obligation clashes with A&B's interests (especially
   patent strategy and future M&A/fundraising).
3. **Escalations** — strategic conditions that are a leadership decision, not a legal one.

Apply every item. For each, state the obligation, then flag conflicts/escalations.

Output shape:
```
Funder / instrument: <name>
Key obligations A&B is accepting:
- <obligation> — <plain implication>
Conflict flags:
- 🚩 <obligation vs. A&B interest> — <why> — <severity>
Escalations (leadership decision):
- ⬆️ <condition> — <why it's strategic>
Confirm with team (👤): <finance/IP/leadership items>
```

---

### 1. Funder & instrument
- **Surface:** which body and programme, grant amount, project duration.
- **Why:** rules differ sharply (national vs EU). Anchors everything else.

### 2. IP ownership & exploitation obligations
- **Surface:** who owns results (usually the beneficiary), and the duties attached — obligation to **exploit** results, and any funder rights.
- **Conflict flag:** exploitation/"use it or lose it" duties, or funder step-in rights, that constrain how A&B commercialises. *Severity: medium–high.*
- **Interacts with:** any research-collaboration agreement on the same project (`research-collaboration.md` #10).

### 3. Access rights (consortium / EU)
- **Surface:** obligations to grant other consortium members access to A&B's background and foreground IP for the project (and sometimes for their own exploitation).
- **Conflict flag:** access rights extending to A&B's valuable background IP, or to results for partners' commercial exploitation. *Severity: high.*

### 4. Dissemination / open-access obligations
- **Surface:** duty to publish/disseminate results; open-access publication and possibly open-data requirements.
- **Conflict flag:** open-access duty **vs. A&B's patent/trade-secret strategy** — mandated publication can pre-empt patent filing. Must reconcile timing (file before you publish; use any protection-of-results / delay provisions). *Severity: high.*
- **Interacts with:** `research-collaboration.md` #7.

### 5. IP-transfer restrictions
- **Surface:** limits on transferring IP or results outside the EU / to non-EU entities, often requiring prior notice/approval.
- **Conflict flag:** restrictions that would bite on a **future acquisition or foreign investment**. *Severity: high.*

### 6. Change-of-control / relocation clawback
- **Surface:** conditions requiring A&B to stay in France/EU, keep production/IP local, or repay if control changes or the company relocates.
- **Escalation:** ⬆️ this is a **strategic/leadership** matter (affects M&A and fundraising), not a pure legal call.

### 7. Sovereignty / security conditions
- **Surface:** for strategic tech (quantum), conditions on foreign takeover, keeping capability in-country, security requirements, sometimes state rights.
- **Escalation:** ⬆️ leadership decision; may need security/defence advice.

### 8. Reporting & milestones
- **Surface:** periodic technical + financial reporting, deliverables, and milestones tied to payment tranches.
- **Conflict flag / 👤:** whether the reporting cadence and milestones are achievable by a lean team — confirm with the project owner. *Severity: medium.*

### 9. Audit rights & eligible costs
- **Surface:** the funder's right to audit costs (often years later); only **eligible costs** may be claimed; ineligible costs are clawed back.
- **Conflict flag / 👤:** finance must be able to track eligible costs and retain evidence. *Severity: medium.*

### 10. Co-financing obligation
- **Surface:** the grant funds a percentage; A&B must co-finance the remainder from its own funds.
- **👤 Confirm:** the cash commitment is budgeted. *Severity: medium.*

### 11. Clawback / repayment triggers
- **Surface:** all events that trigger repayment (missed milestones, misuse, failure of conditions, insolvency, relocation).
- **Conflict flag:** broad or easily-triggered clawback. *Severity: high.*

### 12. State-aid conditions
- **Surface:** public funding is state aid; aid-intensity limits and GBER conditions apply.
- **👤 Confirm:** intensity limits and cumulation with other aid are respected (specialist/finance). *Severity: medium.*

### 13. Consortium obligations (multi-party)
- **Surface:** duties toward other beneficiaries; any **joint and several liability**; the interplay with the consortium agreement.
- **Conflict flag:** joint & several liability for other partners' failures. *Severity: high.*

### 14. Publicity / funding acknowledgement
- **Surface:** obligation to acknowledge funding (e.g. EU emblem) in communications.
- **Why:** minor compliance item; note it.

### 15. Term, amendments & funder termination
- **Surface:** duration, how the funder can amend or terminate, and consequences.
- **Conflict flag:** funder can amend materially or terminate with adverse repayment effects. *Severity: medium.*

---

**Reconciliation reminder:** if there is a related research-collaboration agreement
for the same project, cross-check that its IP, publication, and access-rights terms
are **consistent** with this grant. Conflicts between the two are a common, high-cost
trap — flag them explicitly.
