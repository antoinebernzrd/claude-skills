# Checklist — generic spine (unrecognised in-scope contract)

Use only when the contract is in-scope but doesn't match a specific type. This is a
**best-effort generic pass** — the universal clauses that appear in almost any
commercial contract. **Mark the overall review 👤 "generic pass — human review
recommended" and never conclude "safe to sign as-is."** A specialised checklist,
if one fits, is always preferable.

Apply the A&B lens throughout: IP is the crown jewel, GDPR applies, quantum tech is
export-controlled, and public-funding obligations may lurk in the background.

---

### 1. Parties & capacity
- Correct legal entities named; signatory has authority; A&B entity correct (FR vs US).

### 2. Subject matter & scope
- What each side must do is clear and bounded. Flag vague/open-ended obligations.

### 3. IP ownership
- Who owns pre-existing IP and anything created. **A&B should not lose ownership of, or rights to, its IP.** Flag any assignment/licence of A&B IP, or silence where IP is created for A&B. *Often high/critical.*

### 4. Confidentiality
- Mutual, protects A&B's technical and business information. Flag one-way-against-A&B, residuals, or narrow definitions.

### 5. Personal data / GDPR
- If personal data is processed, GDPR applies; a DPA is needed where one party processes for the other; check EU-transfer basis. Flag absence. *Escalate if processing with no DPA.*

### 6. Liability & indemnity
- Is there a cap? Is it adequate given the downside? Carve-outs for data breach, confidentiality, IP infringement, injury/property. Flag fee-only caps with no carve-outs.

### 7. Warranties
- What each side warrants; watch for A&B over-warranting, or the counterparty disclaiming everything ("as-is").

### 8. Term & termination
- Duration, termination for cause/convenience, and what survives (IP, confidentiality, data return). Flag inability to exit for breach.

### 9. Payment / commercials
- Payment terms, renewals, price changes, and whether payment is tied to performance/acceptance.

### 10. Assignment & change of control
- Can the counterparty assign or be acquired (possibly by a competitor) while holding A&B's rights/information? Prefer consent requirements.

### 11. Governing law & dispute resolution
- Governing law, venue, arbitration. Prefer French/EU or a neutral acceptable forum.

### 12. Export control
- Foreign counterparty + controlled/quantum technical content → export-compliance clause expected; **escalate** if missing.

### 13. Public-funding / sovereignty strings
- Any hint the subject matter touches grant-funded work or strategic-tech conditions → flag for cross-check.

---

**Output:** the standard clause-by-clause format, but open with a clear note that
this was a **generic pass on an unrecognised contract type**, list what could and
couldn't be assessed, and recommend a human review. Do not return an all-clear.
